===========================
Pair-Trade: Is it worth it:
===========================
- correlation
    - strength and direction of linear relationship
    - -1 to 1
    - does not mean assets are cointegrated or tradeable
- half life
    - measures how quickly the spread reverts to its mean
    - half life is expected time it takes for a deviation from the mean to reduce by 50%
    - sharter half life means the sprad mean-reverts faster, attractive for trading
    - estimated using Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process, via linear regression on lagged spread values
- Augmented Dickey-Fuller test (ADF)
    - checks for unit root in the spread
    - null hypothesis: the spread has a unit root (non-stationary) -> not suitable for trading
    - a more negative test statistic than the critical value means we reject H0 (stationary spread)
